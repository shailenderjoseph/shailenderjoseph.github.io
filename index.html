About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at the Naveen Jindal School of Management, The University of Texas at Dallas. My academic advisors are Dr. Vijay Mookerjee, Dr. Ganesh Janakiraman, and the late Dr. Milind Dawande. I am on the academic job market in 2026–27, and I was selected for the ICIS Doctoral Consortium and the INFORMS ISS Doctoral Consortium in 2026.

My research broadly examines three areas: the design of digital platforms and markets, dynamic resource-allocation and pricing decisions, and the design and operation of AI-enabled service systems. Across these areas, I study how firms and platforms make decisions while anticipating the responses of customers, advertisers, and sellers. These themes motivate my current work on real-time bidding in digital advertising, alternative architectures for digital marketplaces, and the cost-effective use of generative AI. In addition, I am working on a research project that examines the economics of cyber-insurance.

Methodologically, I combine analytical modeling with empirical and computational analysis. I draw on game theory, auction theory, optimization, and control theory to characterize equilibrium outcomes and optimal policies, and use data to estimate model inputs, calibrate models, and evaluate their managerial implications.
